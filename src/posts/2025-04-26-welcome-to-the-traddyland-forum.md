1. **Practice Kindness**: Engage with others respectfully, assuming good intentions in all interactions.

2. **Be Supportive**: Offer constructive feedback and encouragement, uplifting others rather than criticizing harshly.

3. **Listen Actively**: Read and consider others’ perspectives fully before responding, showing empathy and understanding.

4. **Avoid Harmful Language**: Refrain from personal attacks, insults, or discriminatory remarks; keep discussions civil.

5. **Resolve Conflicts Peacefully**: Address disagreements with patience and a focus on mutual understanding, not winning.

6. **Give Generously**: Share knowledge, resources, and help freely, contributing to the community’s growth.

7. **Stay Humble**: Acknowledge others’ contributions and admit mistakes graciously, fostering a collaborative spirit.
